Front Review is a process of collaboration between designers and front-end developers. It helps validate solutions made in code and achieve the ideal formula of product development when the design = the code.

## The problem

We had a problem when there were no rules for design review after coding. And that is why some designers did it some did not.

> The process of design review after coding wasn’t transparent and had non-rules.

## The process

To structure this process, I started with interviews with designers and developers to figure out the pain points of the current situation.

We figured out that each product team has its own rules for design review after coding. And some product teams didn’t have this stage at all.

After that, we highlighted the most critical insights from interviews and transformed them into the final solution.

## The solution

The final solution is a process checklist that helps designers to check their designs after coding. There are 2 main parts: delivering design to developers and the front review. The checklist lives in Notion and in Figma.

### Design to Developers

- How to deliver design
- The structure of layouts
- Components
- Margins
- States and errors
- Adaptive

### Front-review

- Checking the solution
- Checking the UX scenarios
- Comments to developer
- Finishing the task

## Results & learnings

The process was beneficial both for product designers and developers. It helped to increase the collaboration in the team. What we already achieved with the process:

- Less amount of UX/UI debts;
- Fewer questions from developers to designers;
- The increasing of the development speed;
- The transparency in processes for product managers.
